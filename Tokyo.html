Tokyo

Tokyo is the capital of Japan and one of the largest cities in the country. It is known for its busy streets, modern buildings, shopping areas, restaurants, and entertainment. At the same time, Tokyo also has many traditional places, such as temples and shrines.

There are many popular places to visit in Tokyo, including Shibuya, Shinjuku, Asakusa, and Tokyo Tower. Visitors can enjoy shopping, trying different kinds of Japanese food, visiting famous landmarks, and experiencing both modern and traditional Japanese culture.
