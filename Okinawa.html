Introdaction

Okinawa is a group of islands in the southern part of Japan. It is known for its beautiful beaches, clear blue water, warm weather, and unique culture. Okinawa has a different atmosphere from many other parts of Japan.

Visitors can enjoy beaches, water activities, and beautiful natural scenery. Popular places include Naha, Okinawa Churaumi Aquarium, and Ishigaki Island. Okinawa is also known for local foods such as Okinawa soba and taco rice.
