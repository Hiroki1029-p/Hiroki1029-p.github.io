About Japan

Japan is an island country in East Asia known for its rich history, unique culture, beautiful nature, and modern cities. From busy cities like Tokyo and Osaka to traditional places like Kyoto, Japan has many different places to explore.

Japan is also famous for its food, including sushi, ramen, tempura, and many local dishes. Visitors can experience traditional temples and shrines, modern technology, beautiful mountains and beaches, and different local cultures throughout the country.


Each region of Japan has its own unique attractions, food, and traditions. Explore this website to learn more about different places in Japan and find information that can help you plan a trip.
