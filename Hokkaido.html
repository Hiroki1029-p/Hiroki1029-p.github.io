Introdaction

Hokkaido is the northernmost main island of Japan and is known for its beautiful nature, mountains, lakes, and snowy winters. It is a popular destination for people who enjoy outdoor activities and natural scenery.

Sapporo is the largest city in Hokkaido and is known for its food, shopping, and winter events. Other popular areas include Otaru, Furano, and Biei. Hokkaido is also famous for seafood, ramen, dairy products, and other local foods.
